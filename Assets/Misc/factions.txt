The Coalition of Innocents
	The local hegemonic military power which keeps peace within the starting system. The coalition was a collection of galactic powers that got its name by refusing to participate in the galaxy wide civil war. After the civil war took place, a few empires were diplomatically absorbed into the coalition but half the galaxy is still under independent/other empire control.

Pirates
	A general term for anyone who flies a ship and steals from others without an empires backing. Pirates can be found within most systems but those further from the central worlds are often easy targets.

The merchants league (find a better name)
	The merchants league, often shortened to "the league." This collection of inner sphere merchants provide safety guides and accurate price reports from every station of theirs. They make a point to construct a station in every system which has more than 2 Billion sapient organisms. They've been known to work with private pilots to clear out pirates threatening important trade routes and to provide defense for the building of stations.
